# Acceptable Use Policy

**Royalton Venture Holding — VENTEX Platform**
**Document ID:** SOC2-AUP-004
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** April 8, 2026
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the standards of conduct for all users of the VENTEX platform. It establishes guidelines for the responsible and secure use of platform resources, protecting both the organization and its clients from security risks and legal liability. This policy supports SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria CC6.1 (logical and physical access controls) and CC1.1 (control environment).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all individuals who access the VENTEX platform, including: Royalton Venture Holding employees and contractors; VENTEX administrators and operators; client organization members with portal access; and third-party partners with authorized access.

## 3. Authorized Use

### 3.1 Permitted Activities

Users may use the VENTEX platform for the following authorized business purposes: managing venture lifecycle operations (creation, tracking, stage advancement); administering SPV deals and investment pipelines; tracking and processing financial distributions; managing tasks, assignments, and deadlines; uploading and managing business documents; using AI-powered agents for venture analysis and recommendations; communicating with team members and clients through platform channels; and configuring organization settings and user preferences.

### 3.2 Role-Based Access

Users must operate within the permissions granted by their assigned role. Attempting to access features, data, or administrative functions beyond one's authorized role constitutes a policy violation.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

### 4.1 Unauthorized Access

Users must not: attempt to access another organization's data or resources; share login credentials, API keys, or session tokens with unauthorized parties; use another user's account without explicit authorization; bypass or circumvent access controls, rate limits, or security mechanisms; or access the platform from unauthorized devices or networks without approval.

### 4.2 Data Misuse

Users must not: export, copy, or transmit confidential data outside authorized channels; store platform data on personal devices or unauthorized cloud services; use client or venture data for purposes unrelated to authorized business operations; or modify, delete, or tamper with audit logs or security records.

### 4.3 System Abuse

Users must not: introduce malware, viruses, or malicious code into the platform; attempt denial-of-service attacks or deliberate system overloading; reverse engineer, decompile, or extract source code from the platform; use automated tools (bots, scrapers) to access the platform beyond authorized API usage; or deliberately trigger rate limits or security alerts.

### 4.4 Content Violations

Users must not upload, store, or transmit content that is: illegal or promotes illegal activities; infringing on intellectual property rights; defamatory, harassing, or threatening; or containing personally identifiable information (PII) without proper authorization and safeguards.

## 5. Security Responsibilities

### 5.1 Account Security

All users are responsible for: maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials; enabling multi-factor authentication when required by their role (mandatory for admin/owner); reporting suspected account compromise immediately; logging out of shared or public devices after use; and using strong, unique passwords that are not reused across services.

### 5.2 Data Handling

Users must: handle data according to its classification level (see Information Security Policy); report any suspected data breaches or unauthorized disclosures; verify recipient authorization before sharing confidential information; and use platform-provided sharing mechanisms rather than external channels for sensitive data.

### 5.3 Device Security

Users accessing the platform must ensure their devices have: up-to-date operating systems and browsers; active antivirus/anti-malware protection; screen lock enabled with reasonable timeout; and encrypted storage for any locally cached platform data.

## 6. AI Agent Usage

### 6.1 Agent Oversight

AI agents (Overdue Scanner, Velocity Tracker, Distribution Monitor, Orchestrator) operate autonomously within configured parameters. Administrators are responsible for: reviewing agent-generated recommendations before acting on them; configuring appropriate auto-advance thresholds and approval gates; monitoring agent event logs for anomalous behavior; and disabling agents that produce incorrect or harmful outputs.

### 6.2 AI-Generated Content

Users must review AI-generated content (draft emails, venture analyses, status reports) before sending or publishing. AI outputs are advisory and do not replace professional judgment, particularly for financial, legal, or regulatory matters.

## 7. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 7.1 Monitoring

The platform monitors user activity through: comprehensive audit logging of all actions; rate limiting with violation tracking; session management with device fingerprinting; and agent event monitoring for automated operations. Users acknowledge that their platform activity is logged and may be reviewed for security and compliance purposes.

### 7.2 Violations

Policy violations are handled according to severity:

| Severity | Examples | Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | Accidental data mishandling, single policy deviation | Warning and remedial training |
| **Moderate** | Repeated minor violations, unauthorized data export attempt | Temporary access suspension, formal written warning |
| **Severe** | Deliberate unauthorized access, data breach, system sabotage | Immediate account termination, legal action if warranted |

### 7.3 Reporting

Users must report suspected policy violations, security incidents, or suspicious activity to their organization administrator or the VENTEX security team. Reports can be made through the platform's notification system or by contacting the security team directly.

## 8. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to address new threats, technologies, or business requirements. Users are notified of material changes and must acknowledge the updated policy.
