# Client Portal Structure (for Demo Client View)

## Key Sections:
1. **Header** — Org name, "Managed by VENTEX", user name, logout
2. **Velocity Summary** — AI-generated weekly summary of what happened
3. **Action Needed (amber cards)** — Items requiring client approval/action
   - Advance stage, approve follow-up, confirm distribution, unblock venture, view tasks
4. **Completed (teal cards)** — Recently completed items
5. **Watching (gray, collapsed)** — Items being monitored
6. **Your Portfolio** — Grid of venture cards with sector icons, stage indicators, progress
7. **Forecasts** — AI predictions for each venture
8. **Market Signals** — Sector-level market intelligence
9. **Portfolio Health** — Overall health scoring
10. **AI Chat (floating)** — Chat bubble with AI concierge

## Key Differences from Admin:
- No sidebar navigation — single scrollable page
- Clean, outcome-focused language — no operator jargon
- Cards are action-oriented (approve, advance, confirm)
- Portfolio view is simpler — just the client's ventures
- AI Chat is a floating bubble, not a full tab
